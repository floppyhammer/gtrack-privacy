Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This App is designed with a "local-first" approach to data processing.

1. Information Collection and Use

  • Sensors & GPS: The App processes high-frequency motion sensor data (accelerometer) and GPS coordinates locally on your device to calculate performance metrics. This data is processed in real-time and is not transmitted to any external servers.
  • Location Data: Location permission is required strictly for speed and distance measurement. We do not track your movement history or store your location logs.
  • Storage Access: When you use the "Save G-Diagram" feature, the App requests permission to write to your media gallery to store the generated image. We do not read or modify any other files in your gallery.

2. Third-Party Services

The App utilizes standard industry providers that may collect data per their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services (Payments & Compliance)
  • AdMob (May use the Android Advertising ID to provide relevant ads)

3. Data Retention & Security

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is no personal data retention. Any historical G-force peaks or settings are stored locally in the App's private storage and are cleared if the App is uninstalled.
